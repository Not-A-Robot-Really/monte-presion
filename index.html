MONTE PRESIÓN
A BLUFFING CARD GAME
A simple card game where four enemies all race against the clock to clear their cards and bluff their way out of their situation at-hand, or die trying.
  • COUNTDOWN: As the timer counts down, drop one card to "claim" it matches (A=1 ... J=11, Q=12, K=13, Joker=Wild) or pass.
  • Call bluff: Hit the button to accuse a rival of bluffing — their top card flips face up. Bluffing? They lose a life. Truth? You lose a life
  • Remove Cards: Instead of placing a card, you can pull one back off your own pile — it counts as your move for that number, but shrinks your pile and clears the suspicion on it.
  • Countown at zero: When the countdown reaches zero, with nobody caught & accused — whoever's placed the fewest cards pays a life.
  • Strategy: Sneak a 3-of-a-kind or run all 4 suits without getting caught, and start next round with a bonus life.
  • 2 lives each: Windows get faster every round. Last one standing wins.
